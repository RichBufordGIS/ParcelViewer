# Credits and acknowledgments

## Project contributions

The Jackson County Public Parcel Viewer was developed through a collaborative effort by the GIS team and supporting county staff. Contributions included project planning, design, application development, testing, and ongoing refinement. The project addressed several operational challenges that previously limited parcel review efficiency, including the transition from weekly parcel updates to nightly updates through Portal-to-AGOL collaboration, a solution developed by Lester Carver. Another key improvement was multi-parcel selection, implemented by Kevin Kandola, which streamlined parcel review and allowed users to assess multiple records more efficiently. These efforts, along with broader team feedback and problem-solving, helped modernize how the Assessment team reviews and displays overlapping land records in a more effective and user-friendly format.
